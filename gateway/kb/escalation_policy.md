## Quick Answer (Escalation Policy)

1. Classify severity (SEV-1 / SEV-2 / SEV-3) based on customer impact and risk.
2. Assign an incident owner (IC) and start a shared incident timeline.
3. Notify stakeholders (Slack / Email / Status Page) according to severity.
4. Start mitigation within 15 minutes for SEV-1; within agreed window for SEV-2.
5. Post updates every 30 minutes (SEV-1) or hourly (SEV-2) until resolved.
